Per workload (median ms)

The two measurements of zoo.js.org, run here on every ticked engine: conformance (1,370 tests of javascript-zoo: ES1–5 pass rate, compat-table's weighted ES6 and ES2016+ rates; about a minute per engine) and speed (the eight Octane v9 suites whose geometric mean is zoo's Score; from under a minute for the browser to a quarter of an hour or more for an interpreter). Every test and suite runs in a fresh realm (for the browser's own engine, a fresh worker). Results are kept in this browser until the engines' build changes.

Scores are higher-is-faster; Score is the geometric mean of the suites that finished, as on zoo.js.org. Engines measured here are highlighted; click a column to sort.
